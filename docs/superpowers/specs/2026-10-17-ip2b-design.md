# IP2b — design

**Interactive Prototype 2b** · Fri 23 Oct 2026, Week 12 Studio · 35 %, **hurdle** · DECO7230
Written 17 Oct 2026 (Sat). Six days to test.

## 1. Intent

The participant should feel **they could turn the room in their head into a real room** — "this is my space and I can reshape it", not "pick a colour from a catalogue".

Two consequences, both Kaike's call:

- **Realism and warmth carry the feeling.** IP2a's rooms read as a test level: mismatched antique furniture, empty floor, a void window. IP2b's rooms must read as a home.
- **Nothing is "staying".** The participant can change anything. This retires the locked concept decision *"match against what's staying"* and the kept/change split. Sampling now works from **any** surface. Evaluation 3 must say so: the hold-up gesture survives, but its reason moves from "match what you keep" to "build a palette from what you like".

## 5. Interactions

### Kept from IP2a (Kaike: "functionality was very good") — don't rebuild

Clock + time of day (`TimeOfDayController`, `WallClock`, F5–F10) · floor material swap with textures · lamp (`LampController`) · furniture grab / move / rotate (`FurnitureSlot`, Q/E) · preset frames · pull affordance tab + glow (`PullAffordance`, `HandGlow`) · hold-up preview/commit (`HoldUpPreviewer`) · controller menu with the full catalogue (`ControllerMenu`) · scheme save/compare (`SchemeManager`, `SchemeCycler`, right A/B — *builder's call: kept, Kaike asked to keep IP2a functionality*) · desktop mode controls · iPad logger.

### Changed / new

| # | Interaction | Behaviour | Replaces / why |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | **Pull from anything** | Every Surface and furniture piece is a sample source. **Furniture is pull-only** (Kaike, 17 Oct): it gives colour, never takes paint; walls + floor take paint. No kept/change marking, no "staying" label, onboarding no longer pulses "kept" surfaces | Kaike: nothing stays |
| N2 | **Fan deck** | After a pull, the sample opens in the hand as a fan of **7 chips, light → dark, always including two off-whites**, derived from the sample. All 7 visible at once. Other hand (or ray) takes one chip; that chip is the held sample | Replaces twist-to-tune (`HarmonyTuner`). Eval 2: hidden options lost to the visible catalogue; people judge on lightness |
| N3 | **Floor tint** | The floor takes a colour from a chip like a wall does, *on top of* its current material (tint), and material swap still works | Kaike: floor = both |
| N4 | **Curtains** | Grab and draw the curtain closed/open. Closing **fades** daylight to dark over ~1 s; lamp + pendant become the light. Opening fades back | New. "Brightness changes" principle: fade, never snap |
| N5 | **Toss** *(first to cut)* | Throw a held chip; on hitting a wall/floor it commits there with a splash. **Both toss and hold-up live at once**; participant chooses freely; logger records which was used per commit; preference asked after (Kaike, 17 Oct) | Eval 2 insight 4 ("throw a balloon at the wall"). A/B against hold-up |
| N6 | **Furniture smoothing** | Smoother move, slower rotation | Eval 2 insight 5 ("too sensitive") |
| N7 | **MR showcase** | **Participant** flips it at the end of the session and paints one real wall; one logged question: *"Could you see yourself using this at home?"* (Kaike, 17 Oct). Runtime toggle → passthrough; detected wall/floor planes become paintable Surfaces; the same chips work. VR is default; toggle back anytime | Shows the concept on a real room. End-of-session showcase, not the main test |

### Decided defaults (builder's call unless Kaike objects)

- **Lightness ladder (N2):** keep `HarmonyPalette`'s hue logic, then order by value; force slots 1–2 to off-whites of the sample hue (very low saturation, value ≥ 0.9). Replace, don't patch, `HarmonyTuner` usage.
- **MR stack (N7):** AR Foundation + Unity OpenXR: Meta (`com.unity.xr.meta-openxr`) for passthrough + plane detection, developed against AR Foundation's XR Simulation environments in the editor. Studio Quest must have Space Setup run before the first participant. If plane detection fails on the day: passthrough-only showcase, or skip.
- **Toggle control:** diegetic, e.g. a "window frame" or the TV — no menu button. Facilitator key in desktop mode.
- **Menu stays** as the full-catalogue alternative, as in IP2a. It now competes with the fan deck, not with twist.

## 6. Testing (plan written separately: `ip2b/2026-10-23-ip2b-testing-plan.md`)

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Platform | **Studio Quest 3S**, controllers. Desktop mode only for the dry run |
| n | ≥ 5, ~5–7 min each |
| Primary questions | (1) Does the fan deck get people choosing from the constrained set vs the menu? (2) Hold-up vs toss: which do people use (logged per commit) and prefer (asked of everyone)? (3) Does the room feel like a home they could make their own? |
| Exposure rule (Eval 2 lesson) | Every decisive question is asked of **everyone exposed** to the thing, whatever path they took. A2 "helpful or restrictive?" asked of everyone who opened a fan |
| Questionnaires (Week 6) | **UEQ-S** (8 items) + **VRSQ** (9 items), after the task. ~2 min |
| Five principles (Week 6 slide 21) | Text readability · Ergonomics · Brightness changes · Avoiding simulator sickness · Button placement — each evaluated, N/A ones explained |
| Research paragraph | 1 paragraph, **≥ 5 academic refs**, tied to IP2b design + testing (own rubric criterion; 0 if missing). Candidates: Hart & Staveland 1988 (TLX), Kim et al. 2018 (VRSQ), Schrepp et al. 2017 (UEQ-S), Milgram & Kishino 1994 (MR continuum), Ishii & Ullmer 1997 (tangible), plus colour/interior-visualisation XR studies |
| Logging | iPad logger (`ip2a/ipad-logger.html`) updated for IP2b tasks + questionnaires; one "next phase" button timestamps phases |
| Submission | Results to GitHub end of class; research summary to Blackboard end of session; AI acknowledgement |
